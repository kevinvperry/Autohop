More reliable listening on Apple TV.

• Improved listening-time accounting during faster playback, seeking and skipping.
• More consistent episode-start, archive and completion statistics, including resumed episodes.
• Stronger local storage and recovery for your listening history and statistics.
• Improved private iCloud history merging and duplicate-subscription handling.
• Clearer Home and Up Next time displays, showing seconds during the final minute.

This update brings shared playback and library reliability improvements to Autohop on Apple TV. Historical listening that was never recorded cannot be recreated.
